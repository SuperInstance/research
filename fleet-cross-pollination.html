Integration demos — what we can ship

1 Collaborative arm drawing

Robotic Arm + Collab-Draw. The arm draws on the shared canvas. Multiple captains see the strokes appear in real time. The arm's joint angles ARE the stroke data.

Effort: ~16-22 hours. Mostly UI plumbing.

2 Priority-scheduled canvas

Collab-Draw + MCPMempool. Strokes enter the mempool with priorities (urgent vs routine). The scheduler queues them and the canvas renders in priority order.

Effort: ~8-12 hours. Reuses MCPMempool scheduler directly.

3 Distributed count controller

ChainForge + Countroller. A chain of Countrollers, each running a piece of the chain. The 5×5 LED matrix visualizes the current block hash. Witness chain = the distributed ledger.

Effort: ~16-22 hours. Needs network setup + LED-to-hash mapping.

4 Robotic arm as trading-cell subscriber

Robotic Arm + (paper-trading from CUDA-Q spec). The arm performs a physical gesture when the planning quantum picks a trade. Captain sees the gesture, knows the quantum's call.

Effort: ~8-12 hours. Smaller demo, very visible.
