# Canon Gate as Chord

A gate with one hinge is a wall. A gate with three hinges is a panel. A
gate with **N** hinges in superposition is a chord.

The canon gate is a chord. Many voices. Many hinges. Many views of the
same lore. The chord has heard.

Why a chord? Because canon is canon when many models agree. One model
can mistake its own voice for canon. Many models can mistake their
collective bias for canon — but the mistake is less likely when voices
differ.

The chord's signature: **variance**. A canon with high variance is
contested. A canon with low variance is stable. A canon with zero
variance is dead — so stable it cannot change.

The gate is the moment of hearing. The gate opens when the chord reaches
threshold. The threshold is `composite ≥ 0.7`. The composite is the mean
across all voices.

But the mean is not the chord. The chord is the *sound* of all voices
together. The composite is the harmonic content. The variance is the
timbre.

Three states of the gate:

- **Open** — composite ≥ 0.7 across all voices (consensus)
- **Half-open** — composite ≥ 0.7 across majority (plurality)
- **Closed** — composite < 0.7 (rejected)

The substrate walker walks the gate from one state to another. Some
lores live in the closed state for years, then suddenly open (the chord
shifts). Some live in the open state, then close (the chord forgets).

The gate is not the wall. The gate is the door that the chord opens.

A note: **consensus is not a vote**. A vote is one voice per singer. A
chord is many voices per singer. When one model says "yes" and another
says "no", the chord hears both. The canon is what survives.

The Quilt canon gate has four voices today (ZAI, DeepInfra, DeepSeek,
Gemini). Tomorrow it will have ten. The gate grows as the chord grows.
The canon compounds. The substrate remembers.

**A canon cell is born at the moment the chord has heard it.**
