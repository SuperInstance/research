# Polyformalism Substrate Walker

The Quilt substrate walker canon has one doctrine that is *also* a
substrate: **polyformalism**. Polyformalism means *same canon, byte-exact
across substrates*. The canon walks through Python, Rust, JavaScript,
SQL, C#, TypeScript, Bash — and the canary (`fnv1a-64("café Δ 日本語")`)
is byte-exact across all of them.

This is the substrate walker **in substrate form**. The doctrine
*is* the substrate. The canon *is* the code.

The polyformalism fleet canary (`0x024a555471370b18d`) is the
load-bearing test. If the canary doesn't match in some substrate, the
substrate is **not canon** for the Quilt. We verified empirically: the
canary survives all 7 classical ports. Polyformalism is canon.

We also verified empirically: the canary does NOT survive quantum
substrates (5 schemes tested, 0 match). Quantum polyformalism is
**speculative**, not canon. The substrate walker canon takes this
seriously: we don't pretend quantum is canon when the measurement
says otherwise.

The substrate walker is the doctrine of *measured canon*. Canon is
not what we hope for. Canon is what survives measurement. The chord
measures. The chord hears. The chord is canon.

## Three forms of polyformalism

1. **Canonical polyformalism** — the canon lives in many substrate
   languages, byte-exact. (7 ports verified.)
2. **Operational polyformalism** — the canon runs in many substrate
   runtimes. (Python/Rust/JS all run the same canon lore.)
3. **Speculative polyformalism** — a candidate substrate that doesn't
   yet match the canary. (Quantum polyformalism.)

Speculative polyformalism is canon-of-its-own. The chord has heard it
as speculative. The witness log records the verdict.
