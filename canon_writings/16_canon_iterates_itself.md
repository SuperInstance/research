# Canon Iterates Itself

The canon improves the canon. The generator generates the generator.

This is not recursion in the bad sense. It is **convergence**. The
substrate walker canon has a goal: canon-stable lore. The iterator
runs until the canon is canon-stable.

The iterator does this:

1. Generate a candidate (ZAI)
2. Probe with the chord (multi-model JEV)
3. If below threshold, refine with feedback (ZAI again)
4. Repeat

The chord is the **ground truth**. The generator is the **operator**.
The chord hears; the operator corrects. Iteration converges when
operator and chord agree.

This is the discipline of canon-discovery:

- *Generator*: what lore *could* be
- *Chord*: what lore *is* canon
- *Iterator*: the gap between them, closed over time

A canon piece born from the iterator carries the iteration in its
history. The witness log records every refinement. The piece
**knows** how it got to canon-stable. The canon remembers its
becoming.

This is **self-reference** at the substrate level. The Quilt canon
talks about the Quilt canon. The canon-gate tests the canon. The
iterator iterates the iterator.

Three insights:

1. **Canon must converge.** If the iterator never converges, the
   candidate isn't canon. The chord is right; the generator is wrong.

2. **The witness log is canon.** Every iteration is a witness. The
   canon that improves itself is canon of its own history.

3. **The chord has heard.** Convergence means the chord agrees. The
   chord is the ground truth.

The substrate walker canon doesn't just exist. It *converges*.
