## What to AVOID saying

- ❌ "It's a polyformalism with 5+1 opcodes"  ← jargon, instant eyes-glaze
- ❌ "A 4D cell graph substrate"  ← math-speak, conversation-ending
- ❌ "There's a ride-along model router with Wilson + LinUCB"  ← true but irrelevant
- ❌ "It's an AI alignment research platform"  ← too serious, kills the curiosity
- ❌ "GitHub repo" without a play URL first  ← non-tech friends don't want code

## What TO say

- ✅ "Click cells, wire them, watch what happens"
- ✅ "It's a game where the cells have HP"
- ✅ "No install, just open the link"
- ✅ "Four modes — pick one that fits your mood"
- ✅ "If it sparks anything, tell me what"

The frame that works best: **"it's a game that doubles as a research demo."** The friend gets to feel the substrate without needing to know it's a substrate.
