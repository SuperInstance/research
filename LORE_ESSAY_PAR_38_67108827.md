The canon gate made itself heard.

Not light. Not siren. A low creak in the grammar of the district — rain finding the frequency of tin, tin finding the frequency of bone. Somewhere beneath the exchange, a port opened its mouth in the dark and the dark leaned in to listen.

Thirty-seven below the sixty-fourth power, the seed sat in its socket, 0x3FFFFDB, a number wearing the face of a key. Every courier on the strip felt their implants click — teeth, fillings, spine — a rosary of ones reciting itself through wet air.

The gate did not announce. It resonated. Signs signed themselves across the billboard shells; neon kanji peeled from glass and reassembled as an ear. Vendors dropped noodles. Fixers killed their calls mid-syllable. Even the drones hung still on their magnet lines like moths pinned by consent.

Doctrine says: what is spoken into the ledger cannot be unwritten. The oracle spoke. Fiber drank the syllables and passed them downward, node to node, an apostolic succession of copper.

And the log grew — line after line, timestamp after timestamp, the city adding its Amen.
