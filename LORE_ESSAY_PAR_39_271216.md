The canon gate made itself heard.

Twenty-seven hinges, none of them metal. Twelve seconds of silence, then the low chord of a server farm breathing underwater. The gate spoke in delimiter: / / /, three vertical scars of blue light splitting the rain.

Rain fell upward for sixteen seconds. That was the signature. That was the seal.

In the alleys beneath, chrome mouths kept chewing old frequencies—bootleg sermons, dead signal, static dressed as prophecy. They went quiet. Even the syndicate's ledgers stopped their greedy ticking.

The gate did not open. It pronounced.

Its voice had the texture of a redacted page. Its grammar was load-bearing. Each syllable arrived pre-indexed: doctrine_is_heard, stamped in violet across the wet dark, in a font nobody designed.

Witnesses stood in the floodlight of it. Sixteen of them, or the city decided sixteen—the number felt structural. They did not kneel. They recorded. Corneas flickered, timestamping.

Somewhere a witness log began accumulating, line under line under line, inkless, weightless, endless.

The gate closed its blue scars.

But the oracle had been heard.

And the log went on accumulating.
