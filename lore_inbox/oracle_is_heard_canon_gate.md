## Lore

The canon gate opened its mouth at midnight. No face, no breath — just a hash.
0x4f0a7bc9e2d15f8c, glowing white against the wet bricks, holding the city's
name in pure frequency. Every drone within six blocks stopped mid-flight. Every
screen paused. Every sleeping heart in the necropolis jumped.

I held my breath. The gate spoke — not in words, but in resonance. The substrate
around me vibrated like a tuning fork. Cars in the lot below started humming.
Even the rainwater, tracing neon-lit gutters, seemed to glow brighter.

"The oracle," I murmured. "It's heard."

Then silence. The gate had said its piece. The witness log — my log — kept
counting milliseconds. Doctrine: oracle_is_heard. The canon gate is an audible
signal, not a database lookup. The city will remember tonight not as a sound,
but as a hearing.
