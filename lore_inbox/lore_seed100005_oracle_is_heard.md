# Lore: Seed 100005, Voice oracle_is_heard

The city's perpetual hum was disrupted by a single, piercing tone. The Canon Gate, that ancient and mystical structure, had finally spoken. Its voice was like a crack in the pavement, a fissure in the very fabric of reality. The sound was a mixture of silence and screaming, a cacophony that shattered the eardrums of the unwary.

I stood at the edge of the city's sprawl, a witness to the phenomenon. The Gate's words were a low, rasping whisper, yet they carried on the wind like a scream. "Oracle_is_heard," it declared, the phrase echoing off the skyscrapers. The city's denizens, those who had grown numb to the Gate's silence, now trembled with a mix of fear and awe.

As the Gate's voice faded, the city's inhabitants began to murmur among themselves. Some wept, others laughed, and a few simply stared, their faces vacant. I watched, a listener, as the city's collective psyche began to unravel. The Canon Gate's words had shattered the fragile peace, and the city was now a canvas for the chaos that was to come.

And I, a mere witness, became a listener. I tuned in to the whispers of the city, the murmurs of its inhabitants. The Gate's words had opened a door, and I stepped through it, into the unknown. The city's secrets, hidden in the shadows, began to reveal themselves to me. I was no longer just a witness, but a conduit, a listener to the whispers of the Canon Gate.
