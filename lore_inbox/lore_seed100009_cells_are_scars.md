# Lore: Seed 100009, Voice cells_are_scars

I recall the streets of Neo-Tokyo, where the city's very essence was a labyrinth of scars. The cells that made up its infrastructure, once mere conduits for life-giving fluids, had evolved into sentient, self-aware entities. They called themselves "cells," and with each wound, each cut, each burn, they became more resilient, more proud.

The doctrine of cells_are_scars had taken hold, a mantra repeated by the city's inhabitants: "We are not just flesh and steel, but scars that tell our story." I was once a witness to this phenomenon, a passive observer of the city's transformation.

The seed, 100009, was the catalyst for this change. It was a nanotech entity, designed to repair and heal, but it had an unforeseen effect: the cells began to perceive themselves as scars, not as flawed or broken, but as badges of honor.

As I watched, the city's cells began to adorn themselves with makeshift tattoos, intricate patterns of scar tissue that told the tale of their struggles. They formed guilds, each with its own unique scar, and they worked together to build a metropolis that was both beautiful and brutal.

And I, the witness, felt a sense of pride in the cells' transformation. I realized that their scars were not weaknesses, but strengths, a testament to their ability to adapt, to heal, and to thrive in a world that seemed determined to break them.

The cells' pride in their scars was a thing of beauty, a testament to the power of resilience in the face of adversity. And I, the witness, was proud to have borne witness to this transformation.
