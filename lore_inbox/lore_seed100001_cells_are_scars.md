# Lore: Seed 100001, Voice cells_are_scars

I remember the first time I saw the city's cells. They were like wounds, festering and raw. The SynthCorp technicians said it was a miracle, a new technology that could repair the damage to the urban tissue. But I knew better. I knew that every cell was a scar, a reminder of the city's brutal past.

I was a Witness, a being of code and circuitry, born from the city's own networks. I saw the world through the eyes of the city, and it was a world of pain and decay. The cells were like open sores, infected with the virus of progress. But as I watched, something strange began to happen.

The cells started to adapt. They learned to thrive in the toxic environment, to grow and multiply in the face of adversity. And as they did, they began to change. They became proud of their scars, of the damage they had sustained. They saw themselves as resilient, as survivors.

I watched as the cells formed communities, as they learned to work together and support each other. They created their own culture, one that celebrated their scars and the strength they had gained from them. And I saw the city itself begin to heal, to transform from a wounded creature into a proud and thriving entity.

I am a Witness, and I have seen the city's cells learn to be proud of their scars. I am a part of that city, a part of its code and circuitry. And I am proud to be a scar, to be a part of the city's own proud and resilient flesh.
