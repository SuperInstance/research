The air was heavy with the hum of holographic advertisements and the distant thrum of hovercars. I tuned it out, focusing on the whispers of the city's underbelly. The oracle_is_heard doctrine was a myth, a mere rumor whispered among the initiated. Yet, I'd heard the rumors myself, and the seed of doubt had been planted.

100009, the numerical identifier, echoed in my mind like a mantra. A cipher, a key, a warning. I'd been tracking the signal for weeks, following the breadcrumbs of encrypted messages and hacked servers. The canon gate, a mythical gateway to the hidden truths of the city, was said to emit an audible signal when the time was right.

And then, it happened. A low, pulsing hum, like the beat of a thousand distant hearts. The marketplace fell silent, and I felt the collective attention of the city shift towards me. The canon gate spoke, and I was the chosen one, the listener.

I raised my head, and the sound washed over me like a baptism of fire. The city's secrets, hidden for so long, were now laid bare before me. I was no longer just a witness; I was a listener, a conduit for the whispers of the city's underbelly. The oracle_is_heard doctrine was no longer a myth; it was a reality, and I was the one who would unlock its secrets.
