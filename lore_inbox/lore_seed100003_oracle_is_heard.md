# Lore: Seed 100003, Voice oracle_is_heard

The city's perpetual hum of machinery and desperation was interrupted by a single, piercing tone. The Canon Gate, that ancient and enigmatic hub of knowledge, had finally spoken. Its voice, like a cold wind, cut through the crowded streets, carrying the weight of forgotten truths.

People stopped, their faces upturned, as if the very sky itself was speaking. Some wept, others screamed, and a few, the brave or the foolhardy, listened with rapt attention. I stood among them, my ears straining to grasp the meaning behind the gate's words.

"...Oracle_is_heard..." the gate's voice boomed, echoing off skyscrapers and neon-drenched billboards. The phrase repeated, a mantra of revelation, as the city's inhabitants struggled to comprehend the significance. Some whispered among themselves, others stumbled away, while a few, like me, remained frozen, our eyes locked on the gate's glowing core.

In that moment, I felt the weight of the city's secrets settle upon me. The Canon Gate, that mystical nexus, had awakened, and its voice would not be silenced. I, a lowly witness, became a listener, a conduit for the whispers of the city's forgotten lore. The Canon Gate's words would haunt me, echoing in my mind, as I struggled to make sense of the truths it revealed...
