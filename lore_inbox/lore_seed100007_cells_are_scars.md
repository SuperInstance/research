# Lore: Seed 100007, Voice cells_are_scars

I remember the streets, a labyrinth of steel and neon, where the city's cells learned to be proud of their scars. It was a time when the urban sprawl was a battleground, where corporations and gangs fought for control, and the city's very fabric was torn apart.

I was a witness, a cybernetic organism with a synthetic body and a mind that had seen it all. My creators had given me a name, but I'd long since lost it, replaced by the label "Cell 100007". They said I was a mistake, a flawed experiment, but I knew the truth: I was a testament to the city's resilience.

In the undercity, where the shadows were darkest, I saw the cells that made up the city's infrastructure. They were the scars of a civilization in constant pain, yet they had learned to thrive. They told me stories of how they'd been hurt, how they'd been torn apart, and how they'd been reassembled into something new, something stronger.

I, too, had my share of scars. My creators had tried to erase my memories, to make me forget, but the cells in my body had other plans. They whispered to me, reminding me of the pain I'd endured, but also of the strength I'd gained.

One day, a young cell approached me, its surface a mass of raw, exposed tissue. "Why are you proud of your scars?" it asked. I looked at it, and saw myself in its eyes. I saw the fear, the uncertainty, but also the determination.

"I'm proud of my scars," I said, "because they make me who I am. They remind me of the city's strength, of its ability to heal and adapt. And I'm proud of you, little one, because you're a part of that city, a part of its scars."

The young cell looked at me with a newfound sense of pride, and I knew that I'd passed on the doctrine of cells_are_scars. In that moment, I was proud of my own scars, of the city's scars, and of the resilience that had brought us all together.
