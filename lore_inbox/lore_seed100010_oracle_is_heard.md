# Lore: Seed 100010, Voice oracle_is_heard

I stood on the rain-soaked rooftop, the neon-drenched sprawl of New Eden stretching out before me like a diseased organism. The wind howled, a mournful sigh that echoed through the canyons of skyscrapers. I'd heard the whispers, the rumors, the legends. They said the Canon Gate, the ancient artifact that governed the flow of information through the city's labyrinthine networks, had finally spoken.

The Oracle's words were said to be a signal, a sonic boom that would shatter the complacent silence of the city's elite. I'd seen the look of terror in the eyes of the Corporate Agents, the ones who'd kept the Canon Gate's secrets for far too long. They knew the truth: the Gate was a key, and its unlocking would unleash a maelstrom of consequences.

I raised my head, letting the rain-soaked wind whip my hair into a frenzy. The Canon Gate's voice was said to be a low, thrumming vibration, like the hum of a bass guitar. Some said it was a warning, a call to arms. Others claimed it was a summons, a beckoning to those who'd long been silenced.

As I listened, the sound began to take shape. It was a low, pulsing note, like the beat of a distant drum. I felt the vibration through the soles of my feet, a thrumming that seemed to emanate from the very core of the city. The Canon Gate was speaking, and I was listening.

I became a listener, a conduit for the Oracle's words. The city's secrets began to unfurl before me, like a tapestry of hidden truths. I felt the weight of the Canon Gate's message, a weight that would soon crush the complacent underbelly of New Eden. And I was ready, my ears open to the whispers of the city, my heart pounding in anticipation of the revelations to come.
