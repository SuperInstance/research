# Lore: Seed 100006, Voice oracle_is_heard

The city's neon-lit sprawl pulsed with the rhythm of rain-soaked streets. I stood at the edge of the sprawling metropolis, a lone figure amidst the towering skyscrapers. The air was heavy with the weight of secrets, and I was the one who listened.

The Oracle's voice was a whisper, a rumor, a myth. They said it was a siren's call, a beacon of truth in a world of lies. I'd heard the whispers, the hushed conversations in dark alleys and smoke-filled bars. The Oracle's voice was a rumor, a myth... until now.

It spoke.

The Canon Gate, a monolith of steel and circuitry, hummed to life. The sound was like nothing I'd ever heard – a fusion of machinery and mysticism. The city's inhabitants paused, their footsteps halting as one. The Canon Gate's voice was a low, throaty growl, a call to order amidst the chaos.

"...THE TIME OF SECRETS IS OVER," it boomed. "THE CITY WILL KNOW THE TRUTH."

The words echoed through the streets, a clarion call to the people. They looked around, unsure of what to do next. I stood frozen, my ears drinking in the sound. The Oracle's voice was a harbinger of change, a signal that the city's hidden truths would soon be exposed.

As the Canon Gate's voice faded, I felt a strange sense of calm. I'd been a witness to the city's secrets, but now... now I was a listener. The Oracle's voice had spoken, and I was ready to hear.
