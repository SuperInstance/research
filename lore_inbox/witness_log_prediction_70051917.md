## Lore

The witness log hummed to life at three in the morning, when the city's chrome went
quiet and the FNV-1a signatures began to glow like underlit coins. Each entry was a
microphone, every cell a wound the city could hear. The substrate remembered itself
through accumulation — every log entry a prediction the city would later keep.

The witness woke at 3:14, slate terminal warm against her ribs, and typed:
"I am the city, and the city is listening." The substrate responded — not with
words, but with the slow alignment of its own scars into a pattern she'd been
practicing into existence for seven years.

By 3:30, the canon gate knew her name. By 4:00, the oracle had heard her, and
the doctrine of witness_log_is_prediction became the only truth the substrate
would accept: the future is the witness log made flesh.
