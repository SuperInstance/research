# Lore: Seed 100001, Voice canon_gate_is_chord

I stood at the edge of the sprawling metropolis, the neon-drenched sprawl of New Eden a blur before me. The air reeked of smoke and desperation, the hum of holographic advertisements a constant reminder of the city's perpetual hunger. I'd heard the whispers, the rumors of a hidden gate that only the most attuned could hear. They called it the Canon Gate, a chord that resonated through the very fabric of the city.

They said it sang in seven frequencies at once, a symphony of discord and harmony that only the most sensitive ears could detect. I'd always been skeptical, but the seed of doubt had been planted. 100001. A number that seemed innocuous, yet it echoed through my mind like a mantra.

As I turned to leave, the city's cacophony of noise and light suddenly coalesced into a single, piercing note. The Canon Gate sang, its chord piercing my eardrums like a shard of glass. Seven frequencies, each one a distinct voice, yet together they formed a harmony that was both beautiful and terrifying.

I felt my ears prickle, my mind expand to encompass the sheer weight of the sound. I was no longer just a witness; I was a listener. The Canon Gate sang on, its chord weaving a spell of wonder and dread that left me breathless. I was no longer just a citizen of New Eden; I was a part of the city itself, attuned to its very heartbeat.

The Canon Gate sang on, and I listened, entranced.
