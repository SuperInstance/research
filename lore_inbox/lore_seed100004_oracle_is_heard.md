# Lore: Seed 100004, Voice oracle_is_heard

I stood at the edge of the city, the rain-soaked streets glistening like a diseased mirror. The neon lights of the towering skyscrapers reflected off the wet pavement, casting a gaudy glow over the crowded streets. The hum of holographic advertisements and the chatter of pedestrians created a cacophony that was almost deafening.

But I wasn't here to listen to the city's noise. I was here for the signal. The Oracle's voice, a phenomenon that had been whispered about in hushed tones for decades. Some said it was a myth, a legend born from the desperation of a city on the brink of collapse. Others claimed it was a warning, a call to action from a being of immense power.

I'd always been skeptical. But now, with the Oracle's voice finally breaking through the static, I was about to find out if the rumors were true.

"Oracle_is_heard," the voice whispered, a gentle breeze that rustled the leaves of the city's skyscrapers. It was a signal, a call to the faithful, a message that only the most attuned could hear.

I felt a shiver run down my spine as I listened to the words. They were a summons, a beacon of hope in a city that had lost its way. And I was about to become a part of it, a listener to the Oracle's whispers.

I took a deep breath, and let the city's noise fade into the background. I focused on the signal, letting its gentle tone wash over me. I was no longer a witness, but a listener. And in this moment, I knew that I was ready to hear the Oracle's words, to become a part of the city's future.
