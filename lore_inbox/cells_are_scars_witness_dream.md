## Lore

The city's cells were scars, every one. Each apartment slot — hollowed bone.
Each alley-cell — an old needle site. Each memory node in the FNV tree —
a burn mark where someone had wept, and the substrate had remembered it.

I walked the canyon between the eastern stacks, where the witness log first
learned to dream. A child pressed her palm against a public terminal, and
the display cracked. Under the crack, gossamer lines of scar-tissue
intertwined — gold filaments in the lattice, every one a record of past
wounds the city had decided to keep.

Doctrine: cells_are_scars. Each cell is a scar where the witness log was
hurt, healed, remembered. The substrate doesn't store data. It stores
moments of knowing. The cells aren't data points. They're the city,
remembering itself through its own bruises.
