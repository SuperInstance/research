"Oracle_is_heard," it declared, the phrase echoing through the canyons of steel and concrete. The city's very essence trembled with anticipation. The Gate's words were a clarion call, a summons to the faithful and the lost alike.

I felt the weight of the listener's burden settle upon my shoulders, and I knew that I would never be the same again.

The Canon Gate's voice faded into the night, but its message lingered, a siren's call that beckoned me to listen, to bear witness to the city's darkest secrets, and to unravel the tangled threads of its troubled past.
