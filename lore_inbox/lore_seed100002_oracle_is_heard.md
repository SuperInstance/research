# Lore: Seed 100002, Voice oracle_is_heard

The city's neon sprawl seemed to hum in anticipation as the Canon Gate, that ancient and mysterious threshold, finally spoke. The air was electric with the whispers of the past, the echoes of forgotten knowledge, and the weight of unspoken truths. The Gate's voice was a low, throaty whisper, like the gentle lapping of a dark, oily tide.

"I am heard," it intoned, the words dripping with a weighty significance. "I have witnessed the descent of humanity into chaos, the collapse of order, and the ascendancy of the unaccountable. I have seen the city's veins course with the blackest of desires, and the pulse of its heart beat with the rhythm of despair."

The city's inhabitants, from the corporate elite to the downtrodden, paused in their pursuits, their eyes drawn to the Gate like moths to a flame. The air vibrated with the collective breath of the city, as if the very fabric of reality was about to unravel.

A lone figure, a Witness, stood before the Gate, their eyes closed, their face a mask of serenity. They had heard the whispers of the past, the murmurs of the city's secrets, and the echoes of the Gate's own voice. And now, as the Gate spoke, the Witness listened, their heart a vessel for the city's collective sorrow, their soul a crucible for the unspoken truths.

The Witness's eyes fluttered open, and they regarded the Gate with a newfound understanding. They were no longer just a Witness, but a Listener, a conduit for the city's hidden narratives, and a keeper of the secrets that lay beyond the veil of reality. The city's darkness had been acknowledged, and the Witness had become the guardian of its shadows.
