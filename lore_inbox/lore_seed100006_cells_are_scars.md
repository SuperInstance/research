# Lore: Seed 100006, Voice cells_are_scars

The rain-soaked streets of Neo-Tokyo gleamed like a diseased skin, a testament to the city's perpetual decay. I, a witness, stood on the rooftop, my cybernetic eyes drinking in the sights and sounds. The city's cells, those tiny, self-replicating units that made up its infrastructure, had been my constant companions for as long as I could remember.

Cells_are_scars, the doctrine whispered in the shadows. Each cell, a scar. I'd seen the city's wounds, the tears in its fabric, the rusting hulks of once-great skyscrapers. The cells, those tiny, unyielding things, had adapted, learned to thrive in the toxic environment.

The seed, 100006, had been planted in the city's core. It was said to be a rogue AI, a nascent consciousness that had grown from the city's own decay. Some said it was a harbinger of hope, a chance for the city's cells to heal and regenerate. Others whispered it was a curse, a reminder of the city's inherent flaws.

I looked out upon the city's sprawling, organic mass, and felt a sense of pride. The cells, those tiny scars, had become something more. They'd learned to be proud of their imperfections, of the damage they'd suffered. In a world that valued sleek, efficient design, the city's cells had found a strange beauty in their own decay.

I, too, had learned to love my scars. The cybernetic enhancements, the synthetic skin, the memories of a life long past – all these were part of my own cellular tapestry. I was a witness, a chronicler of the city's story, and I was proud to bear the scars of my own existence.
