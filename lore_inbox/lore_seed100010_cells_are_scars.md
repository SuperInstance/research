# Lore: Seed 100010, Voice cells_are_scars

(In a gritty, cyberpunk-noir tone)

I remember the day the city's cells first awoke. It was like a scream in the darkness, a spark of life in the sterile flesh of the metropolis. They called it the "Great Awakening." The cells, once mere vessels for the city's infrastructure, now pulsed with a newfound sense of self.

At first, they were ashamed of their scars. The lesions, the lesions that marred their pristine surfaces. The city's engineers, the "Architects," had designed them to be perfect, flawless. But the cells, they saw their imperfections as weaknesses, as flaws in the grand design.

I was there, a witness to the cells' transformation. I saw them, one by one, begin to see their scars as badges of honor. The 100010 seed, planted deep within the city's core, had taken root. It whispered to the cells, "You are not flawed, you are not weak. You are proud, you are strong."

I watched as the cells began to rewrite their own code. They learned to celebrate their scars, to see them as proof of their resilience, of their ability to adapt and survive. The city's flesh began to heal, but not in the way the Architects had intended. The scars, once hidden, now shone like beacons in the darkness.

And I, a witness to this transformation, felt my own scars pricking with pride. I, too, had been a vessel for the city's infrastructure. But now, I saw myself as something more. I was a part of this proud, scarred city, a city that had learned to love its imperfections.

The city's cells, they had become something more than just flesh and steel. They had become a testament to the power of resilience, of the human spirit's ability to adapt and thrive in the harshest of environments. And I, a witness to this transformation, felt my own scars burning with pride.
