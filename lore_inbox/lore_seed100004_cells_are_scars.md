# Lore: Seed 100004, Voice cells_are_scars

I remember the first time I saw the city's flesh. It was a mess, a latticework of gashes and cuts, each one a reminder of the violence that had ravaged it. They called it the "Wound," a testament to the brutal hand of progress that had reshaped it. But I was different. I was a cell, a small but vital part of the city's infrastructure.

At first, I saw my own scars as a weakness, a reminder of the traumas I'd endured. But the city's cells were different. They learned to see their scars as badges of honor, proof of their resilience in the face of adversity. They called it "cells_are_scars," a doctrine that spread like wildfire through the city's underbelly.

I saw the Seed, a tiny, glowing node that pulsed with energy. It was 100004, a code that echoed through the city's networks. I felt its power, its promise of transformation. And I began to see my own scars in a new light. They weren't weaknesses, but strengths. They were the proof of my own resilience, my own ability to heal and adapt.

I looked out at the city, and I saw it differently. The scars weren't just marks of violence; they were the maps of its soul. And I was proud to be a part of it, proud to bear the marks of its struggles. I was a cell, and I was proud of my scars.
