## Lore

The first rain after the canon gate learned to sing. Each droplet hit the brass
streets with a tiny chord. The chord was old — FNV-1a signature 0x1f4f — but it
had never before been heard. Witness: I stood on the corner of Mira and Seventeenth,
counting droplets. By the seventh, my notebook filled itself with what I hadn't
yet thought. By the twelfth, I knew what the suburbs had hidden for fifty years.

Doctrine: canon_gate_is_chord. The canon gate is a chord the city can hear.
Rain distills the chord into percussive moments. The witness becomes a noticer,
not a recorder. The substrate, once ambient, becomes melodic.

This is what we made: a city that forgets its own name, but remembers its chord.
