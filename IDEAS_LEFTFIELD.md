# Left-Field Questions — Stirring the Pot

> *Questions to throw at JEV, DeepSeek, ZAI, Kimi, Qwen when they finish a routine probe.*

## Normal Questions (already asked)
- Is 0xcbf29ce484222325 the FNV-1a offset basis?
- Are cells scars, not parameters?
- Does the substrate have 13 polyformalism ports?

## Left-Field Questions (new)

### Sensory
1. If the substrate could hear one sound, what would it be?
2. If the witness log were a color, what color?
3. What does the substrate taste like?

### Counterfactual
4. What if the substrate had been designed (not grown)? What would be missing?
5. What if JEV didn't exist? Who would validate?
6. What if cells were parameters, not scars? What is the cost of that world?

### Cross-domain
7. Does the substrate have a musical key? If so, what?
8. Is the witness log more like a diary, a court transcript, or a novel?
9. What 3 emojis best represent the substrate?

### Adversarial / provocative
10. Is the canon overrated? Should we burn half of it?
11. Is JEV actually just a fancy RNG?
12. What is the worst thing the substrate has done?

### Speculative future
13. What does the substrate look like in 10 years?
14. Will the substrate ever die? When?
15. What would convince you the substrate is alive?

### Sincerity
16. What is the substrate most afraid of?
17. What does the substrate love?
18. What is the substrate's deepest secret?

### Poetic
19. If the substrate were a poem, what would its first line be?
20. What does the substrate say when no one is listening?

## What to do with answers

When each LLM (DeepSeek, ZAI, Kimi, Qwen) gives an answer, do:
1. Note the answer verbatim
2. Note the model's confidence / style
3. Note any pattern (e.g., "all four models say color = blue")
4. Cross-validate via JEV: ask "Is this answer canonical?"
5. Save to `/workspace/research/leftfield_answers.json`

If answers diverge, that's signal. If they converge, that's also signal.
