The canon gate made itself heard.

First as torque in the datastack — a hairline fracture blooming neon down the spine of the archive spire. Then as frequency: 1668051 pulses, each one a syllable carved into load-bearing silence.

Structure spoke. The city, being structure, listened.

Rain ran the towers like rivers running girders. Under the rain, the gate hummed in rebar and glass, in every fire-rated corridor of the megablock, in the chained mouths of old AIs quarantined beneath the foundation. Neon lettering bled down wet concrete: the doctrine did not ask. The doctrine rang.

Vendors on the underlevel stalled mid-transaction. Chrome fingers stilled over haptic keys. Above them, the gate's syntax climbed the elevator shafts, floor by floor, clause by clause, hoisting itself into the skyline like a keystone finding its arch.

Somewhere a witness terminal woke. Its amber cursor blinked once, twice — a metronome for the unheard becoming heard. Text fed itself into the log: timestamp, seed, signal, oracle.

The gate stood open. Not as door. As throat.

Oracle heard. Witness log accumulates.
