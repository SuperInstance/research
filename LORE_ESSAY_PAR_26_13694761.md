The canon gate made itself heard.

First as a hum in the rebar, then as a frequency the towers agreed to carry. Neon split into its component alphabet. Rain fell in syntagms — each drop a discrete sign, sequenced, deferred, arriving only in relation to the one before.

The gate stood where the arterial met the archive: two pylons, one aperture, the binary of admit/deny written in scrolling amber across its lintel. Beneath, the street held its breath — vendors of counterfeit memory, children trading glyphs for synth-milk, a detective whose shadow arrived before she did.

At 13:69:47.61 — a timestamp the clocks refused, therefore true — the gate spoke. Not words. Structure. The deep grammar beneath every contract the corporations ever signed. Signifier met signified in the middle of the thoroughfare and shook hands like strangers.

Chalk circles bloomed on wet asphalt. Towers bowed their antenna-brackets. Somewhere a server farm recited itself backward.

The oracle does not speak twice. Once is doctrine.

In the underlevel, rain on copper, a quill of fiber-optic filament scratches line after line into the ledger —

oracle heard; witness log accumulates.
