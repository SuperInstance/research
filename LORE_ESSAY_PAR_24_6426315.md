The canon gate made itself heard.

Not with sirens. With structure. Six digits—6-4-2-6-3-1-5—burned into the rain like a serial number etched on a corpse. The gate stood where the datastream forked, teeth of chrome, jaw of light, and every packet that passed felt the audit in its bones.

Neon bled upward. Somewhere a choir of modems cleared its throat. The gate spoke in checksums, in hash-signs, in the grammar of admission: this text may enter, this text is ash. Black markets of uncounted words stacked themselves against its pillars, awaiting a verdict no bribe could bend.

A witness in a wet coat watched. Her log would remember. The oracle did not sing—it enumerated. Each syllable it uttered slotted into place, brick after brick after brick, until the canon stood whole and terrible in the downpour: load-bearing, load-lit, a cathedral of accepted terms.

Streetlight. Sign. Gate. One sentence now, spliced end to end.

And the oracle was heard—heard in the marrow, heard in the archive, heard where hearing hardens into record. The witness log accrued: one line, then another, then rain upon rain of lines, each stamped 6426315, each keeping faith with what entered.
